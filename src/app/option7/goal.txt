Gift Shop Angle
USP: "The best thing you can send someone who has everything"

Structure: Hero copy targets the gifter, not the recipient — "Know someone who deserves something handmade?" Soft photography-style layout (warm tones). Product cards include a "Perfect for:" tag (e.g., "Perfect for: your Discord mod, your work bestie, your tired mom"). Reviews are framed as gift reactions. Mailing list pitch: "We'll remind you before holidays and special occasions."

Vibe shift: Completely reframes the customer — someone buying for someone else. Opens a different emotional lane and could drive gifting occasions.
