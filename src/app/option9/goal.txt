Seasonal / Moment in Time
USP: "Right now, this is what's in the oven"

Structure: The hero changes with the season — right now it might say "Spring batch is here: lemon cookies & strawberry brownies." Below: a "What's happening right now" section that combines the live stream status, current shop items, and a pinned Discord message. Everything is framed as ephemeral and current. Mailing list CTA: "Get notified when the season changes."

Vibe shift: Creates a sense that the site is alive and always relevant to now — rewards repeat visits rather than one-time browse.
