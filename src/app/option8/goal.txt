Meet the Baker
USP: "You know who baked this. That's the whole point."

Structure: Opens with a personal intro section — photo/video of Merr, a few sentences about her story in first person, handwritten-style. Then: "Here's where to find me" (stream, socials) and "Here's what I make" (shop). Reviews appear as letters, styled with a paper-texture card and a wax seal icon. Very little grid; mostly flowing single-column with generous whitespace.

Vibe shift: The person is the product. Builds parasocial warmth before the shop ever appears. Works especially well for audience members who discovered her through Twitch.
